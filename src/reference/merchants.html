FAQ

Common questions from partners.

Is it free to register as a merchant?

Yes — creating your business profile and listing offers on Offsay is free. Reach out via our contact page for details on any optional promoted placements.

How long does verification take?

Our team typically reviews new listings within a few business days. You'll be notified as soon as your business goes live.

Which areas does Offsay currently support?

We're rolling out district by district. Register through the merchant portal and we'll confirm coverage for your location.

Can I manage multiple locations?

Yes, multi-location businesses can register each branch as a separate listing from the merchant portal.

Who do I contact for support?

Visit our Contact page and our team will get back to you.
