What is Offsay

One app for every offer worth knowing about.

No more following ten different store pages or missing a sale because it wasn't advertised near you. Offsay pulls every nearby, active offer into a single, filterable feed.

Discover nearby

Set your distance radius — 10, 20, 30 km — and see only offers you can actually reach today.

Every category

Food, bars, fashion, footwear, salons, electronics and more — filter to exactly what you're shopping for.

Save & track

Bookmark offers for later, rate the ones you've used, and get a heads-up before a deal expires.

How it works

Finding a deal takes three taps.

1

Set your location

Turn on location, choose a radius, and Offsay finds every active offer within reach.

2

Filter by category

Browse trending or most-liked offers, or jump straight to a category like footwear or salons.

3

Redeem in-store

Get directions with one tap, show your offer at checkout, and rate it once you're done.
